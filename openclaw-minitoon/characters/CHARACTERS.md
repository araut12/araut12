# Minitoon characters

Reference pictures live in `~/.openclaw/workspace-minitoon/characters/samples/`.
Pass 1-3 of them as `character_refs` in `episode.json` so the Gemini images keep the character on-model.

## Bunny (main character)
- Look: small cream/ivory fluffy bunny, long ears with pink insides, big round blue eyes, rosy cheeks,
  two little front teeth, yellow T-shirt with a red star, blue shorts. Sometimes a light-blue backpack.
- Personality: curious, cheerful, a little shy at first, always kind.
- Family: Mommy Bunny (taller, cream fur, pink apron).
- Friends seen so far: a yellow duckling, a brown puppy with a blue bandana.
- Best references: `samples/sample-04.jpg` (front view, bedroom), `samples/sample-07.jpg` (full body, backpack),
  `samples/sample-01.jpg` (waking up).
- Places: blue bedroom with round window, bathroom with yellow tiles, cozy kitchen, pink cottage with flowers,
  flower path, playground.

## Boy (second character)
- Look: little boy with messy black hair, big brown eyes, yellow T-shirt with a smiling sun, blue shorts,
  red-and-white sneakers.
- Reference: `samples/sample-10.jpg` (backyard with tree house, swings and garden).

## Style (use for every episode)
3D Pixar-style animation, cute chibi proportions, big expressive eyes, soft fluffy textures,
bright saturated colors, warm sunny lighting, cozy detailed background, child-friendly, no text.
