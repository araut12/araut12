# Minitoon characters

The user attaches their Bunny reference picture in the Gemini app, so prompts should describe the
action and place. Don't re-describe Bunny's look in a way that conflicts with the picture.

## Bunny (the star) - a girl: she / her
- Look (current design, from the user's pictures): small fluffy GREY bunny with a white muzzle and
  tummy, long upright ears with pink insides, big shiny eyes, a pink collar with a little flower charm.
  Sometimes wears a small backpack.
- Reference picture: `characters/bunny-ref.jpg`
- Personality: curious, cheerful, a little shy at first, always kind. Loves singing.
- Family: Mommy Bunny (taller grey bunny, pink apron).
- Friends: Duckling (small yellow duckling), Puppy (brown puppy with a blue bandana).
- Places: flower garden path, pink cottage with a thatched roof, playground with a rainbow slide,
  cozy kitchen, blue bedroom with a round window.

## Style (every episode)
3D Pixar-style animation, cute chibi proportions, big expressive eyes, soft fluffy textures,
bright saturated colors, warm sunny lighting, cozy detailed background, child-friendly, no text.

## Older design (not used now)
A cream bunny in a yellow star T-shirt (`characters/samples/`). Use it only if the user asks.
